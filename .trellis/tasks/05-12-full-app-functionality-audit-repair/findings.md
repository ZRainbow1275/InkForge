## Severity Scale

- `critical`: data loss, fake success for durable operations, native blocker, or route-breaking crash.
- `high`: primary user workflow broken or Settings persistence incorrect.
- `medium`: secondary workflow broken, accessibility/keyboard issue, or recoverable service error not surfaced.
- `low`: polish issue with no functional loss.
- `info`: planning or evidence note.
