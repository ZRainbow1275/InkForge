Market Editor CloakBrowser Field Study - 2026-06-19

Scope:
- Real headed CloakBrowser observation of 135 SVG editor and Xiumi studio authoring surfaces.
- Rule extraction only; no account credential, login artifact, capture bundle, local runtime path,
  or third-party asset address is recorded here.
- No platform proof was claimed from these observations.

135 SVG editor observations:
- The SVG editor exposes left-side categories for click, carousel, slide, auto, long-press,
  layout, official-account, mini-program/link, and other interaction families.
- Selecting a free-trial material-included effect inserted a center-canvas block named for a
  cover-click/move/spread interaction family.
- The publish-like visual layer was a zero-font and zero-line-height section containing a
  background-only SVG with a 1080x1920 viewBox, width 100%, vertical-align top, margin-top -1px,
  background-size just over 100%, user-select disabled, and pointer-events disabled.
- The clickable/editable trigger was not inside that SVG. It was an authoring overlay with absolute
  percentage inset values and a visible switch for showing the trigger hot area.
- The right-side parameter panel exposed structured controls for cover image material, movement
  direction, cover animation duration, expand animation duration, and editable expanded content.

Xiumi studio observations:
- The SVG category lists behavior families such as basic SVG, image carousel, click expand, path
  animation, drawing/lottery, slide trigger, parallax movement, click switch, page flip, zoom,
  click answer, text barrage, click show/change/open/disappear/pop/zoom/print/jump/play,
  long-press switch, area trigger, drop, and click plus auto.
- Visible SVG items include descriptive metadata: component family, behavior, interaction, and
  image-ratio hints such as 1080-based ratios.
- Clicking a visible SVG carousel/scrolling item inserted content into the center editor, but the
  active authoring DOM did not expose literal inline SVG. It materialized as nested article, page,
  layer, group, image, text, and container cells.
- The inserted authoring model used tn-cell-type, tn-child-position, tn-child-orientation,
  tn-animate, contenteditable text cells, raw-image cells, fixed and flow-canvas orientations,
  and nested sortable groups.

InkForge rules extracted:
- Market editor observations can inform InkForge-owned schema: background media slots, hotspot
  regions, trigger type, motion timing, direction, expanded content, image-ratio manifests,
  fallback poster/long-image plans, and phone-preview proof requirements.
- 135/Xiumi authoring wrappers and metadata must remain market-editor residue blockers in
  publishable output.
- Background-only SVG with image layers should be modeled as a safe SVG/image fallback pattern
  only when InkForge owns the generated markup and commits redacted, safe artifacts.
- Xiumi's SVG labels are catalog metadata, not a direct inline-SVG availability signal.

Boundary:
- This does not prove WeChat ordinary Ctrl+V paste, authenticated PC editor DOM readback, phone
  preview, mobile SMIL/click behavior, mobile Dark Mode, cover thumbnail acceptance, credentialed
  sync, scheduled send, public host acceptance, XHS/Zhihu account upload, or publish success.
