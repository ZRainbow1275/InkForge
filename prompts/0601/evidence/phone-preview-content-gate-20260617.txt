Phone Preview Content Gate - 2026-06-17

Scope:
- Local executable proof-gate hardening for redacted StyleProofManifest records.
- Prevents scan, entry, setup, or cover-setting states from satisfying mobile-preview readback.
- No platform mutation, phone preview opening, sync, upload, schedule, or publish action was
  performed.

Implemented:
- Added StyleProofArtifact.phonePreviewContentVerified?: boolean.
- Strengthened phone-preview-readback validation for mobile-preview evidence.
- Added style-proof-manifest-phone-content-missing for phone-preview artifacts that do not prove
  final article-body content readback.

Behavior:
- A phone-preview-readback artifact must still use action phone-preview, channel phone-preview,
  and phone or visual readback.
- It now also needs phonePreviewContentVerified:true before it can satisfy
  phone-preview-readback.
- A scan page, preview entry, setup dialog, cover-setting page, or PC backend DOM readback remains
  setup/blocking evidence only.
- The rule preserves the old readback-missing status when no phone-preview artifact exists at all.
- The rule does not change style availability, selectable, usable, blocked, or unavailable states.

Regression coverage:
- A phone-preview entry/readback artifact without final article-body content verification is
  invalid and surfaces style-proof-manifest-phone-content-missing.
- Screenshot, Dark Mode, and cover-thumbnail proof rows remain independently satisfied when their
  own artifacts are present, but they cannot replace final phone content readback.
