- Editor reachability, DOM count, SVG count, and toolbar visibility are therefore preconditions,
  not fidelity proof.

  `style-proof-manifest-editor-mojibake-not-ruled-out`.

  with `mojibakeFreeVerified:true`.

  `pc-editor-dom-readback`, without promoting or completing phone, sync, publish, public-host, XHS,
  or Zhihu gates.
