Xiumi Audio Panel Residue - 2026-06-29

Scope:
- Local static publishability protection for copied or partially cleaned Xiumi v5 paper-editor
  audio/video library and page background-music panel chrome.
- No platform action, no phone preview, no sync, no upload, no publish.

Live DOM source:
- CloakBrowser read the live Xiumi v5 paper editor DOM at the logged-in paper editor surface.
- The sampled DOM exposed source-specific audio/media panel markers:
  - audios
  - audio-panel
  - audio-src
  - audio-status
  - audio-edit
  - audio-del
  - audio-group
- The live nodes are editor controls for choosing music, editing or clearing audio, and toggling
  page background music state. They are not article body DOM, platform-safe media embeds,
  upload manifests, or target-platform proof.

Implemented:
- Added `Xiumi audio panel residue` to `MARKET_EDITOR_RESIDUE_RULES`.
- The rule matches class/id attributes containing:
  - audios
  - audio-panel
  - audio-src
  - audio-status
  - audio-edit
  - audio-del
  - audio-group
- The rule is ordered after hidden media upload input handling and before account/sync panels, so
  audio-library chrome keeps an exact diagnostic while upload and sync controls remain
  independently attributable.
- Added three-platform regression coverage for WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and Zhihu.

Negative scope:
- The rule does not block ordinary prose or article markup containing audio, music, video, panel,
  source, edit, delete, status, background, library, or media wording by itself.
- The rule does not block ordinary `<audio>` elements without one of the Xiumi class/id markers.
- The reduced fixture does not rely on hidden media upload inputs, generated-link controls,
  account/sync panels, Angular runtime classes, hosted media, sidebar controls, or meta panels.
