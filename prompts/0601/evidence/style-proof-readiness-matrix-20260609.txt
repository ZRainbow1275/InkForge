Runtime contract

- The API creates one empty style-choice draft per style choice on the requested platform.
- Every draft has artifacts: [].
- Each row carries:
  - the style choice.
  - the empty draft.
  - the getStyleProofManifestReport() result.
  - blockedByCatalog.
  - missingRequirementIds.
  - invalidRequirementIds.
- The platform summary counts total rows, valid proof rows, catalog-blocked choices, missing
  requirements, and invalid requirements.
- Because every row starts from empty artifacts, valid proof row count should be zero until real,
  redacted artifacts are supplied through a future evidence collection path.
