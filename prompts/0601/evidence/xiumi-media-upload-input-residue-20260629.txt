Xiumi Media Upload Input Residue - 2026-06-29

Scope:
- Local static publishability protection for copied or partially cleaned Xiumi v5 paper-editor
  hidden audio/video upload controls.
- No platform action, no phone preview, no sync, no upload, no publish.

Live DOM source:
- CloakBrowser read the live Xiumi v5 paper editor DOM at the logged-in paper editor surface.
- The sampled DOM exposed source-specific editor-side markers:
  - audioFileUploadInput
  - videoFileUploadInput
  - tn-audio-uploader
  - tn-video-uploader
- The live inputs are hidden `type=file` controls with audio/video accept lists. They describe the
  editor file-selection surface, not article body DOM, article media embeds, or platform upload
  proof.

Implemented:
- Added `Xiumi media upload input residue` to `MARKET_EDITOR_RESIDUE_RULES`.
- The rule matches class/id attributes containing:
  - audioFileUploadInput
  - videoFileUploadInput
  - tn-audio-uploader
  - tn-video-uploader
- The rule is ordered after generated-link controls and before account/sync panel rules so hidden
  media upload inputs keep an exact diagnostic.
- Added three-platform regression coverage for WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and Zhihu.

Negative scope:
- The rule does not block ordinary prose or article markup containing audio, video, upload, file,
  input, media, mp3, m4a, mp4, or mov wording by itself.
- The reduced fixture does not rely on generated-link controls, account/sync panels, Angular
  runtime attributes, hosted media, sidebar controls, or meta panels.
