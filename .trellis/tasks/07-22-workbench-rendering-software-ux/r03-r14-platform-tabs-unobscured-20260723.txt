Root cause
- At desktop widths `.panel-inspector:not(.pinned)` is an absolute right-edge overlay with
  `top: 0` and `z-index: 90`.
- The only platform switch is the existing `.stage-platform-tabs` in `.stage-header`; no duplicate
  platform switch exists in the Inspector.
- The overlay geometry therefore hid the authoritative switch rather than the platform styles
  being absent from the registry.
