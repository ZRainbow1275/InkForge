- No WeChat draft creation, edit, save, preview, upload, sync, schedule, publish, deletion, or

Reusable helper added:
- `inkforge/scripts/probe-windows-foreground-input.ps1`

Helper behavior:
- Restores and foregrounds a matching Chromium window.
- Clicks a caller-supplied screen coordinate, or derives one from viewport defaults.
- Sends `Ctrl+V` or a diagnostic `A` key.
- Supports both:
  - `SendInput` for regression comparison.
  - `KeybdEvent` for the working Windows key path.
- Supports `-PreserveClipboard` so a previously prepared Windows `HTML Format` payload is not
  overwritten before Ctrl+V.

  local probe. The final probe used a visible local `about:blank` controlled page whose OS window
  title and DOM target were aligned.

Decision:
- The local non-Playwright ordinary OS Ctrl+V keyboard channel is now usable when:
  - CloakBrowser visible OS window and controlled DOM target are aligned.
  - The click coordinate is calibrated from the visible window.

  - `-PreserveClipboard` is used after preparing CF_HTML.
