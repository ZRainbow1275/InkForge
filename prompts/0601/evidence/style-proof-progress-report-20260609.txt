Scope
- Added `getPlatformStyleProofProgressReport(platform, manifests)`.
- Inputs are caller-supplied redacted `StyleProofManifest` objects.
- The report merges only existing artifacts for the same platform and style choice.
- The report does not create proof artifacts and does not change style availability.

Runtime Contract
- Every style choice is evaluated in `style-choice` scope through `getStyleProofManifestReport()`.
- Gate aggregation reuses the same gate order as the collection plan and queue:
  local-evidence, sensitive-hygiene, market-editor, authenticated-pc-editor, phone-preview,
  public-host, credentialed-channel, platform-publish.
- Cross-platform or unknown-choice manifests are excluded for the requested platform and counted as
  ignored.
- Blocked or unavailable choices remain visible as progress rows, but they are not promoted to
  usable, selectable, uploaded, previewed, synced, scheduled, or published.
