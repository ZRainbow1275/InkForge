Publish Center Rich-Copy Sanitizer Hardening - 2026-07-04

Scope:
- Local application proof for the Publish Center rich-copy fallback payload.
- WeChat SVG/style copy readiness only.
- No XHS/Zhihu publish automation.
- No WeChat phone preview, credentialed sync, scheduled send, public rendering, or publish.

Implemented:
- Added src/services/export/publish-copy.ts.
- Exported PUBLISH_COPY_ALLOWED_TAGS, PUBLISH_COPY_ALLOWED_ATTR, and
  sanitizePublishRichCopyHtml().
- PublishView now calls sanitizePublishRichCopyHtml() in the execCommand fallback path.
- The fallback now fails closed when document.execCommand('copy') returns false.
- The fallback now removes the temporary DOM node and clears the selection in finally.

Sanitizer behavior:
- Preserves current WeChat-safe SVG subset needed by flagship presets:
  data-ink-svg, data-ink-block, inline svg, viewBox, width, height, geometry, paint,
  typography, transform, and safe SMIL timing attributes.
- Removes scriptable or non-WeChat-safe constructs after DOMPurify:
  script tags, style tags, foreignObject, event-handler attributes, and javascript: URI values.

Regression coverage:
- New publish-copy.test.ts feeds a real flagship Markdown-to-WeChat render through
  sanitizePublishRichCopyHtml().
- The test proves real body text remains readable after flagship drop-cap styling.
- The test proves data-ink-svg, inline svg, data-ink-block, width="100%", and viewBox survive.
- The same test injects hostile script/style/event/foreignObject/javascript payloads and proves
  they are removed.
- PublishView.wechat-presets.test.ts now proves the component uses the shared sanitizer and checks
  the execCommand fallback return value.
