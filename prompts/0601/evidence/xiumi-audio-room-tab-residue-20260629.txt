Xiumi Audio Room Tab Residue - 2026-06-29

Scope:
- Local static publishability protection for copied or partially cleaned Xiumi v5 paper-editor
  audio/video library room-tab chrome.
- No platform action, no phone preview, no sync, no upload, no publish.

Live DOM source:
- CloakBrowser read the live Xiumi v5 paper editor DOM at the logged-in paper editor surface.
- The sampled DOM exposed source-specific audio/video room tab markers:
  - tn-aud-rooms-tab
  - tn-aud-room-item
- The live tab items are editor controls for switching between system music, personal music, and
  personal video rooms. They are not article body DOM, platform-safe media embeds, upload
  manifests, published media proof, or target-platform playback proof.

Implemented:
- Added `Xiumi audio room tab residue` to `MARKET_EDITOR_RESIDUE_RULES`.
- The rule matches class/id attributes containing:
  - tn-aud-rooms-tab
  - tn-aud-room-item
- The rule is ordered after hidden audio-library control handling and before visible audio-panel
  chrome, so upload inputs, hidden media controls, audio-room tabs, and visible audio panel
  controls remain independently attributable.
- Added three-platform regression coverage for WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and Zhihu.

Negative scope:
- The rule does not block ordinary tab/list markup.
- The rule does not block ordinary prose about audio, music, video, rooms, tabs, media, upload, or
  library wording by itself.
- The rule does not block ordinary `<audio>` elements or standard Bootstrap `nav nav-tabs` classes
  by themselves.
- The reduced fixture does not rely on parent `audios` wrappers, audio panel controls, hidden
  audio-library controls, hidden upload inputs, generated-link controls, account/sync panels,
  Angular runtime attributes, hosted media, sidebar controls, or meta panels.
