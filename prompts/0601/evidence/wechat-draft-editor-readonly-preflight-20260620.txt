Rule impact:
- Existing draft editor reachability can support `authenticated-editor-url` and readonly
  `pc-editor-dom-readback` preconditions only when the evidence is redacted.
- It cannot satisfy paste, exact artifact, safe draft cleanup, phone preview, Dark Mode, cover
  thumbnail, credentialed sync, scheduled send, platform preview, public article rendering, or
  publish rows.
- Replacement-glyph/mojibake readback is a hard visual badcase. It must be recorded as negative
  fidelity evidence and must not be promoted into a successful WeChat style proof.

Boundary:
- This proves only that an authenticated existing WeChat draft editor surface and its main body
  DOM were reachable in read-only mode on the local PC.
- It does not prove that any InkForge export artifact was pasted, preserved, cleaned up, previewed
  on phone, accepted by cover thumbnail, synced, scheduled, publicly hosted, or published.
