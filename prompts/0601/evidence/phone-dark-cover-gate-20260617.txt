Phone Dark Mode and Cover Thumbnail Gate - 2026-06-17

Scope:
- Local executable proof-gate hardening for redacted StyleProofManifest records.
- Prevents ordinary phone screenshots or cover setup screenshots from satisfying Dark Mode or
  cover-thumbnail acceptance.
- No platform mutation, phone preview opening, sync, upload, schedule, or publish action was
  performed.

Implemented:
- Added StyleProofArtifact.darkModeEnabledVerified?: boolean.
- Added StyleProofArtifact.coverThumbnailAccepted?: boolean.
- Strengthened dark-mode-check and cover-thumbnail-check validation.
- Added style-proof-manifest-dark-mode-not-verified.
- Added style-proof-manifest-cover-thumbnail-not-accepted.

Behavior:
- dark-mode-check still requires action dark-mode-check, channel phone-preview, and
  phone/screenshot/visual readback.
- It now also needs darkModeEnabledVerified:true before it can satisfy Dark Mode proof.
- cover-thumbnail-check still requires action cover-thumbnail-check, channel phone-preview, and
  phone/screenshot/visual readback.
- It now also needs coverThumbnailAccepted:true before it can satisfy cover thumbnail proof.
- The rules preserve missing status when no matching readback artifact exists at all.
- The rules do not change style availability, selectable, usable, blocked, or unavailable states.

Regression coverage:
- A manifest with phone body readback and phone screenshot proof remains invalid when the Dark Mode
  artifact does not prove mobile Dark Mode was enabled.
- A cover setup screenshot remains invalid when it does not prove platform preview-entry/share/list
  acceptance.
- Phone readback and phone screenshot rows remain independently satisfied when their own artifacts
  are present.
