Helper behavior:
- Reads a UTF-8 HTML artifact from disk.
- Wraps the exact artifact as a Windows HTML clipboard fragment with `StartHTML`, `EndHTML`,

- `-DryRun` computes the exact CF_HTML payload metadata without changing the clipboard.

Dry-run command shape:
- `powershell.exe -NoProfile -STA -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File inkforge/scripts/set-windows-html-clipboard.ps1 -HtmlPath <artifact> -DryRun`
