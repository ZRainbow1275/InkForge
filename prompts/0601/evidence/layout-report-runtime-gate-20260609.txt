Implementation:

- Added `wechat-layout-report-required` in
  `inkforge/src/services/export/quality-detector.ts`.
- The detector now reports this error when WeChat publishable output still contains:
  - free positioning (`position:absolute/fixed/sticky`)
  - z-order layers (`z-index`)
  - background image layers
  - cropped overflow (`overflow:hidden/clip`)
  - fixed geometry
  - manual offsets
  - negative overlap spacing
  - invisible/custom hit areas
- The gate is intentionally separate from `wechat-unsupported-css`.
- Normal InkForge-owned flow blocks with `background-color`, borders, padding, margin, and
  readable line-height are covered by a negative-control test and do not trigger the gate.
