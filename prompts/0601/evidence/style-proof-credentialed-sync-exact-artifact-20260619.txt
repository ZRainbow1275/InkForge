Style Proof Credentialed Sync Exact Artifact Contract - 2026-06-19

Scope:
- Local style-proof contract hardening for credentialed sync/channel proof rows.
- No platform action, no editor open, no phone preview, no sync, no upload, no scheduled send,
  and no publish action.

Implementation:
- Strengthened `credentialed-channel-response` and `sync-readback` execution contracts so both
  require `exactArtifact:true` in addition to `artifactFingerprint`,
  `externalAccountAuthenticated:true`, and `safeForCommit:true`.
- Added validator checks that emit `style-proof-manifest-exact-artifact-missing` when an
  authenticated credentialed response or sync readback is not bound to the exact exported artifact.
- Strengthened execution-runbook failure signals so account responses, upload responses, draft ids,
  or material readbacks for a different artifact cannot prove this exported artifact was synced.

Regression coverage:
- Runbook rows for `credentialed-channel-response` and `sync-readback` expose `exactArtifact` as a
  required field.
- Authenticated credentialed sync artifacts without `exactArtifact:true` keep both requirements
  invalid and surface `style-proof-manifest-exact-artifact-missing`.
- Existing external-account authentication and scheduled-send/publish gates remain separate.
