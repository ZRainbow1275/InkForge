  event, PC DOM readback, and sensitive-artifact hygiene.

  thumbnail inspection, exact artifact, and sensitive-artifact hygiene.
- Zhihu image-fallback style-choice proof requires both public image host proof and a validated
  Zhihu artifact manifest proof entry. XHS artifact manifest proof is treated the same way for XHS
  choices that require it.
