Style Proof Artifact Manifest Validation - 2026-06-19

Scope:
- Local style proof validation for XHS and Zhihu artifact-manifest requirements.
- No platform action, phone preview, upload, sync, scheduled send, or publish action.

Implemented:
- StyleProofArtifact now supports artifactManifestValidated.
- xhs-artifact-manifest and zhihu-artifact-manifest execution contracts require:
  - artifactRef
  - artifactManifestValidated
  - safeForCommit
- validateStyleProofManifest now rejects manifest-shaped proof rows unless the row also proves the
  matching platform manifest validator passed.
- The execution runbook next action, success criteria, and failure signals name the matching
  platform validator so operator checklists do not drift from validator behavior.
- The committed XHS local evidence manifest sets artifactManifestValidated:true only for the
  committed validator-passed manifest report.

Behavior:
- A local artifact with kind artifact-manifest, action artifact-manifest-validation, and readback
  manifest is still insufficient by itself.
- Missing artifactManifestValidated:true produces:
  style-proof-manifest-artifact-manifest-not-validated.
- The flag may be set only after validateXhsImageArtifactManifest() or
  validateZhihuImageArtifactManifest() returns no issues for the exact redacted manifest being
  referenced.
- The flag does not prove account upload, platform preview, public article rendering, sync,
  scheduled publish, or publish success.

Regression coverage:
- XHS cover-carousel proof with unit/local/exact/hygiene rows and a manifest-shaped proof row stays
  invalid when artifactManifestValidated is absent.
- Committed XHS local evidence still satisfies xhs-artifact-manifest because it references the
  validator-passed committed report and sets artifactManifestValidated:true.
- The execution runbook exposes artifactManifestValidated for XHS and Zhihu artifact-manifest rows.
- The execution runbook points XHS artifact-manifest rows at validateXhsImageArtifactManifest() and
  Zhihu artifact-manifest rows at validateZhihuImageArtifactManifest().
