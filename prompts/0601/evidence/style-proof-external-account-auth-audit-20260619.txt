- A credentialed-channel or platform-publish shaped proof row that omitted positive
  externalAccountAuthenticated:true could keep the requirement-level manifest status invalid, while
  the acceptance audit still classified the row as unsafe-to-automate.
- That wording was too weak: a proof row without positive account-authenticated readback is concrete
  invalid proof, not merely an operator action waiting to run.

Implemented:
- Added style-proof-manifest-external-account-auth-missing to the acceptance-audit invalid issue set.
- Kept ordinary missing external gates on the existing blocked-by-external / unsafe-to-automate path.
- Kept PC editor readback gaps that require a human/operator account action on the existing
  unsafe-to-automate path unless they carry a concrete invalid issue already classified by the audit.
