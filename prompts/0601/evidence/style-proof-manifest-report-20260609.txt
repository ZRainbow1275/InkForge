Runtime contract

- validateStyleProofManifest(manifest) remains the authoritative validator and returns QualityIssue[].
- getStyleProofManifestReport(manifest) reuses the validator result and groups it into:
  - requirement rows: satisfied, missing, invalid.
  - artifact rows: accepted, invalid, sensitive, unsafe-commit.
  - summary counters for required proof items, missing/invalid proof, accepted artifacts,
    sensitive artifacts, unsafe committed artifacts, and total issue count.
- The report is intended for redacted proof manifests, docs, completion reports, and follow-up
  CloakBrowser platform probes.
- The report must not be used to upgrade blocked choices, infer mobile preview, infer sync,
  infer publish, or treat account/editor reachability as paste success.
