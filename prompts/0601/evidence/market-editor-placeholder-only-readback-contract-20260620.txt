Market Editor Placeholder-Only Readback Contract - 2026-06-20

Scope:
- Local style-proof manifest validation and acceptance-audit hardening.
- This slice converts the 135/Xiumi applied-editor learning boundary into an executable contract.
- No platform action, account upload, phone preview, sync, scheduled send, publish, screenshot,
  network capture, QR material, credential material, browser runtime artifact, or private editor
  source is recorded here.

Implemented:
- `StyleProofArtifact.marketAppliedContentVerified?: boolean`
- `style-proof-manifest-market-editor-placeholder-only`
- `market-applied-dom-readback` now requires the same matching market-editor row to carry:
  - `centralEditorChanged:true`
  - `marketAppliedContentVerified:true`
  - accepted DOM or visual readback
  - `safeForCommit:true`

Behavior:
- A 135/Xiumi list/category/item click that does not mutate the center editor remains invalid via
  `style-proof-manifest-market-editor-not-applied`.
- A center editor/canvas change that is only listing-only, placeholder-only, no-material, or lacks
  meaningful DOM/controls/slots/visible content is invalid via
  `style-proof-manifest-market-editor-placeholder-only`.
- The requirement-level acceptance audit maps that issue to `invalid` and exposes it in
  `cannotClaim`.
- The hygiene row `no-proprietary-template-source` remains independently satisfiable when its own
  source-hygiene artifact is valid; the placeholder failure is scoped to `market-applied-dom-readback`.
