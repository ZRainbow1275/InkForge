Extracted WeChat market taxonomy:
- background SVG and static/raster fallback layers.
- click reveal, click switch, click hide/show, and multi-step reveal.
- carousel, fade, slide, long-image, and auto/gesture transition families.
- trigger zones stored as normalized regions rather than copied vendor overlays.
- card, title, divider, cover, image-slot, and text-slot structures.
- path animation, parallax, quiz/game, marquee, and lightweight interactive poster families.

InkForge rule mapping:
- Market taxonomy can create or enrich InkForge-owned module families only.
- Vendor DOM, builder classes, data attributes, hosted material URLs, and account/editor residue
  must not be copied into production output or committed proof.
- WeChat SVG/H5-inspired choices stay blocked until exact InkForge-owned artifacts pass the
  required local, phone, Dark Mode, cover, public, sync, and publish gates.
- H5 is treated as an external/landing-page capability, not a claim that an Official Account article
  body can run arbitrary script or full web-app logic.

Xiaohongshu boundary:
- Rich authoring can be converted into image/card artifacts.
- Publishable body remains platform-native text plus image/card outputs.
- HTML, arbitrary CSS, raw SVG, and WeChat decorations must not be treated as XHS body proof.

Zhihu boundary:
- Publishable body remains clean semantic Markdown plus platform/public hosted image fallback.
- Arbitrary HTML, raw SVG, unhosted local image references, and WeChat interactive wrappers remain
  blocked until the required public-host or platform-host proof exists.

Boundary:
- This is public-source taxonomy and conservative boundary documentation only.
- It does not prove WeChat official editor paste, phone preview, mobile interaction, Dark Mode,
  cover thumbnail acceptance, credentialed sync, public-host acceptance, Xiaohongshu/Zhihu account
  upload, scheduled send, platform preview, public rendering, or publish success.
