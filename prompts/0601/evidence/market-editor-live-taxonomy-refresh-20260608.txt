135 Editor:
- URL checked: `https://www.135editor.com/beautify_editor.html`
- DOM/title signal: `135编辑器官网-AI微信排版编辑器-微信公众号图文排版工具，好用素材多`
- Current taxonomy confirmed:
  - editor workflow: `导入`, `插入`, `一键排版`, `复制`, `保存`, `同步`, `预览`, `公众号长图`
  - toolbar controls: default font, clear format, format brush, font size, bold, italic,
    underline, delete line, text/background color, alignment, first-line indent, paragraph
    spacing, line spacing, letter spacing, side padding, image upload, quote, link, Word-image
    upload, AI polish/generate, fullscreen writing mode
  - style families: title, body, image-text, guide, layout, festival, industry, small element,
    SVG
  - platform/permission states: authorized official account, scheduled send, watermark,
    full-text format, team management
  - preview states: 5.5-inch plus, 5.5-inch, 5-inch and below, dark-mode preview, temporary
    preview/share link
- Engineering conclusion:
  - 135 remains a taxonomy/workflow reference, not source code or template input.
  - `copy`, `save`, `sync`, `preview`, `authorized-account`, and `scheduled-send` must remain
    separate artifact states in InkForge.
  - Dark Mode preview is an evidence target, not proof that InkForge SVG text is safe on phone
    WeChat.

Xiumi:

- DOM/title signal: `图文: (无标题) | 秀米XIUMI`

  - permission gates: sync preview requires WeChat certification; comment options require
    official-account comment permission; plugin/sync paths are separate from copy/paste
  - material groups: theme color, title, card, image, layout, SVG, component, article template,
    image material, favorites, clipboard, personal gallery, team material, audio/video,
    sample template
  - SVG/action families: carousel, click reveal, path animation, draw/lottery, fun slide,

    bullet text, click show, click change image, click open, click disappear, click popup,
    click zoom, click print, click jump, click play, long-press switch, region trigger, click

  - layout/layer controls: background image, background repeat, background color, transparency,
    positioning, z-order, spacing, line spacing, letter spacing, page margin, scaling, component
    positioning, page alignment, multi-select alignment, advanced width/height/X/Y position
  - mobile risk: background image height over 4000 can fail on Android phones; some media cover
    interactions require plugin/sync upload; images can be too tall to click reliably
- Engineering conclusion:
  - Xiumi confirms InkForge's `editor-workflow-system` and `layout-and-layer-system` separation.
  - Action/SVG families remain taxonomy unless InkForge owns the renderer and has real platform
    proof.
  - Free layout, z-order, backgrounds, media covers, and large background images require
    layout reports and raster/long-image fallback, not raw WeChat HTML assumptions.
