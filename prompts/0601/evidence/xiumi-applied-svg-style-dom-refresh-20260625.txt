InkForge rule translation:
- Xiumi SVG/H5 effects can materialize as a mixed authoring component tree with inline SVG,
  `foreignObject`, SMIL-like animation nodes, flow-canvas wrappers, editable text cells, runtime
  bindings, and hosted media references.
- Do not copy Xiumi `tn-*`, `ng-*`, `opera-*`, `contenteditable`, flow-canvas, component binding,
  hosted media, or preview-card source into InkForge runtime output.
- Translate the observed idea into InkForge-owned primitives only:
  - deterministic viewBox and responsive shell geometry
  - image-slot manifests for gallery/page-turn content
  - action/trigger manifests for click/page-turn/sequence behavior
  - static-expanded fallback and raster fallback when the target platform cannot prove the
    interaction path
  - layout reports for absolute/free-canvas compositions
  - residue blockers for copied market-editor authoring DOM
- The observed `foreignObject` and SMIL-like nodes do not weaken the WeChat-safe subset. They stay
  blocked or fallback-gated until exact platform proof exists.
- A market style click is useful proof only when central editor content changes and meaningful
  applied content is read back. Center-unchanged title/card/listing clicks remain taxonomy evidence
  only.
