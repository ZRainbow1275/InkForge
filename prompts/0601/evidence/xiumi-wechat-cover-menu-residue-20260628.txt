Xiumi WeChat Cover Menu Residue - 2026-06-28

Scope:
- Static publishability protection for copied or partially cleaned Xiumi v5 WeChat cover menu and
  cover preview controls.
- No platform mutation, no upload, no sync, no schedule, no phone preview, no cover thumbnail
  acceptance, and no publish action.

Source:
- A live CloakBrowser Xiumi v5 DOM readback exposed cover-menu controls such as a class value
  containing both `op-bar-menu` and `cover-menu`, cover preview wrappers such as
  `op-ce-video-xm-cover`, and child preview markers such as `svg-cover`.
- Co-observed child classes `cover-desc` and `cover-imgs` are documented as context only and are
  not standalone detector triggers.
- No account-state material, local browser runtime material, capture-file reference, platform
  publish artifact, export artifact, copy artifact, sync artifact, preview artifact, or QR artifact
  is part of this evidence.

Implementation:
- Extended the existing `Xiumi WeChat cover control residue` detector to catch:
  - a class/id value containing both `op-bar-menu` and `cover-menu`;
  - `op-ce-video-xm-cover`;
  - `svg-cover`;
  - existing `op-ce-wx-cover`.
- Tightened the generic `Xiumi operation bar dropdown residue` detector so `cover-menu` is
  classified under the more specific WeChat cover-control label instead of emitting both labels.
- Added a three-platform regression fixture proving the reduced cover-menu child DOM is blocked
  for WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and Zhihu without relying on generated-link, dark-mask, or generic
  operation-bar diagnostics.
